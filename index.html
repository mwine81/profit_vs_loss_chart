Dollar Variance Between Discounted AWP Price and NADAC

Positive values show discounted AWP price above NADAC acquisition cost; negative values sit below NADAC.

Dollar variance equals Discounted AWP Price (dx_amt) minus NADAC Acquisition Cost. Values to the left of $0.00 pay below NADAC; values to the right pay above NADAC. Extreme upper-tail drugs above the display cap are grouped into the final overflow bin.
